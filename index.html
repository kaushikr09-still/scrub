Scrub

Masks personal details in your text before you paste it into an AI chatbot, then puts them back into the chatbot's reply.

Reduces what you expose. It does not guarantee anonymity.

Everything runs in this page. Your text is not sent or saved anywhere. Reloading the page forgets it.

⚠ Names not checked yet

Names, organisations and places are NOT checked yet (rules only).

Downloads about 110 MB once, then stays on this device. Your text is never sent.

The rules look for: email addresses, phone numbers, Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN, IFSC, UPI IDs, card numbers, dates, money amounts, PIN codes, bank account numbers (only next to words like A/c or Account No), web addresses (URLs) and IP addresses.
