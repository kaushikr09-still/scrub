Model bake-off (dev only)

Runs each model in bakeoff/candidates.json over the sentences in bakeoff/names.json. Models are downloaded from the model host; no text of yours is involved. Names are scored two ways (see below the table).

Not started.

Each cell shows two numbers per group of 9 sentences. "touched": any person prediction overlaps the name. "fully": every letter of the name sits inside a person prediction after snapping to whole-word edges (the same snapping the page applies). Size is measured from the files the loader reports (1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes); the reported size is the claim in candidates.json. ms per sentence is the average over all sentences after one untimed warm-up run, on this machine's CPU. "Person outside name" counts person predictions (word pieces joined) that do not overlap the name. No confidence floor is applied.
